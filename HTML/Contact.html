Frequently Asked Questions

Here are some common questions about our services:

How long does it take to find a home?

The time it takes to find a home depends on your preferences and the current market conditions. However, our team works closely with you to ensure a smooth and efficient process.

What is your typical closing timeline?

Our typical closing timeline is 30-45 days for cash buyers and 45-60 days for financed buyers. However, this can vary depending on the property and your individual circumstances.

Do you work with international buyers?

Yes, we work with international buyers and have extensive experience in assisting clients from around the world.

Do you charge a commission on sales?

Yes, we charge a commission on sales. The commission rate varies depending on the property and the services required. Please contact us for more information.

Can I view properties before making an offer?

Yes, you can view properties before making an offer. We recommend scheduling a viewing in advance to ensure the property is available and that we have a representative available to show it to you.
